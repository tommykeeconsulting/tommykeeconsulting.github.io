Privacy Policy

Sudoku Bakery: Crumb Crew · Effective November 10, 2026

Sudoku Bakery: Crumb Crew ("the app") is made by Tommy Kee. This page explains what information the app uses and why. In short: the app itself collects nothing about you, has no accounts and does no tracking. Free players see ads from Google AdMob, which are described below.

What the app does not do

  • No sign-up, account or login.
  • No analytics, and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We have no server, so your games, settings and photos are never sent to us.

Your games and settings

Your games, progress and settings are stored on your device. If iCloud is turned on, they sync between your own devices through your iCloud account, which Apple runs. We can't see that data.

Photos and the camera

If you use Bake from a photo, the app reads the puzzle from the picture on your device. The photo is never uploaded. The app asks for camera or photo access only when you use this feature, and you can change that permission at any time in your device's Settings.

Sharing puzzles

When you send a puzzle to a friend, the app makes a picture of the puzzle with a QR code. The code holds only the puzzle's starting numbers and the name you choose to sign it with. You decide who to send it to through your device's Share sheet.

Purchases

Purchases and the free trial are handled by Apple through the App Store. We don't receive your payment details.

Ads (free players on iPhone and iPad)

Free players see ads served by Google AdMob: a banner in the game screen, and short ads that play only when you tap a button marked "Ad". Anyone who has bought the full game, or is in the free trial, sees no ads. The Mac version has no ad service.

  • The app asks Google for non-personalised ads only. That means ads aren't chosen from a profile of you, and the app never asks for permission to track you.
  • To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's ad service may collect information such as your device's IP address, device and app details, and how you interact with ads. Google describes this at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, and in its Privacy Policy.
  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent message asks for your choices before ads are shown. You can change them at any time in the app under Settings › Ad privacy choices.
  • Ads are limited to content suitable for general and family audiences. Categories such as gambling, dating and alcohol are blocked.

Children

The app is a puzzle game for a general audience. It doesn't knowingly collect personal information from children, and the app itself collects none from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
