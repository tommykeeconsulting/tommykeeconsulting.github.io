Privacy Policy

Sudoku Bakery: Crumb Crew · Effective November 13, 2026

Sudoku Bakery: Crumb Crew ("the app") is made by Tommy Kee. This page explains what information the app uses and why. In short: the app itself collects nothing about you and has no accounts. Free players see ads from Google AdMob, described below; those ads are personalised only if you allow tracking when the app asks.

What the app does not do

  • No sign-up, account or login.
  • No analytics of our own. The app itself never tracks you across other companies' apps or websites; Google's ad service may, but only if you allow it (see Ads).
  • We have no server, so your games, settings and photos are never sent to us.

Your games and settings

Your games, progress and settings are stored on your device. They are included in your device's own backups (iCloud Backup or a backup on your computer), which Apple and you control. The app doesn't sync them between devices yet, and we can't see them.

Photos and the camera

If you use Bake from a photo, the app reads the puzzle from the picture on your device. The photo is never uploaded. The app asks for camera or photo access only when you use this feature, and you can change that permission at any time in your device's Settings.

Sharing puzzles

When you send a puzzle to a friend, the app makes a picture of the puzzle with a QR code. The code holds only the puzzle's starting numbers and the name you choose to sign it with. You decide who to send it to through your device's Share sheet.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We don't receive your payment details.

Ads (free players on iPhone and iPad)

Free players see ads served by Google AdMob: a banner in the game screen, and short ads that play only when you tap a button marked "Ad". Anyone who has bought the full game sees no ads. The Mac version has no ad service.

  • Tracking only with your permission. On iPhone and iPad, free players are asked once, through Apple's standard prompt, whether to allow tracking. If you allow it, Google may use your device's advertising identifier to show ads that suit you better. If you don't, the ads are non-personalised: they aren't chosen from a profile of you. The game works the same either way.
  • You can change your answer at any time in your device's Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google's ad service may collect information such as your device's IP address, device and app details, and how you interact with ads. Google describes this at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, and in its Privacy Policy.
  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent message asks for your choices before ads are shown. You can change them at any time in the app under Settings › Ad privacy choices.
  • Ads are limited to content rated for general audiences with parental guidance at most (no mature content). Categories such as gambling, dating and alcohol are blocked. To report an ad, see Report an ad.

Children

The app is a puzzle game for a general audience. It doesn't knowingly collect personal information from children, and the app itself collects none from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
