Common questions

How do I remove the ads?
Buy the full game: in the app, open Settings and tap Get the full game, or tap any "Go ad-free" link. It's a one-time purchase with no subscription. It removes all ads (no videos, no banner) and adds every Special board theme, more hints on Tricky and Expert puzzles, all three crumb trails with 15 crumbs each, and as many photo puzzles as you like. One purchase covers your iPhone, iPad and Mac, and it can be shared with your family through Family Sharing.

I bought the full game, but the app still shows ads or locked features.
Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.

How do I move my games to a new device?
Your games, progress and settings are kept on the device you play on. When you set up a new iPhone or iPad from a backup of the old one (iCloud Backup, or a backup on your computer), they come along. Syncing between your devices is planned for a later update. The full game itself follows your Apple Account: on another device, open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases.

Bake from a photo didn't read my puzzle correctly.
Fill the camera's square with the puzzle, in good light and as flat as you can. You can fix any number on the next screen before you start. A photo from your library or a screenshot works too.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?
On iPhone and iPad, the free version asks once whether to allow tracking. You can change that answer at any time in your device's Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, you can also open Settings in the app and tap Ad privacy choices. The full game and the Mac version have no ads, so they show no ad choices.

Report an ad

If you see an ad that seems inappropriate, misleading or broken, please email tommy.kee.consulting@gmail.com with a screenshot of the ad if you can, roughly when you saw it, and which device you were using. We'll ask Google to block it. Ads in the game are limited to content for general audiences with parental guidance at most, and categories such as gambling, dating and alcohol are blocked.
