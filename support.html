Common questions

How do I remove the ads?
Buy the full game from the "Go ad-free" link in the game. It's a one-time purchase with no subscription. It removes all ads and unlocks every crumb trail and every Special theme. One purchase covers your iPhone, iPad and Mac, and it can be shared with your family through Family Sharing.

I bought the full game, but the app still shows ads or locked features.
Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.

Is there a free trial?
Yes. After you've finished a few puzzles, the app offers 7 days of the full game for free. Nothing is billed, nothing renews and there's nothing to cancel. When the trial ends, the app goes back to the free version.

How do I move my games to a new device?
Turn on iCloud for the app on both devices, using the same Apple Account. Your games, progress and settings sync between them.

Bake from a photo didn't read my puzzle correctly.
Fill the camera's square with the puzzle, in good light and as flat as you can. You can fix any number on the next screen before you start. A photo from your library or a screenshot works too.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?
In Europe, the UK and Switzerland, open Settings in the app and tap Ad privacy choices.
